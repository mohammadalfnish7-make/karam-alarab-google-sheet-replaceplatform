# Process

## Ordering company expenses

This covers company expenses such as stretch film, pallets, security tips, and car oil.

1. Mostafa starts the order and informs Amer.
2. Amer communicates with the supplier. The people who work in these fields are Indian, so Amer can get a better price with a discount.
3. Qusai is responsible for the payment. Cash payments stay within the 2000 DH limit.
